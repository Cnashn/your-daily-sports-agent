# 12 August 2026

We went to Graz and the two-goal lead became meaningless the moment the whistle went. Sturm pressed from the first minute because they had to, because home advantage and the reputation that comes with a 13-game unbeaten run in the league meant nothing if they rolled over. So they came forward, we compressed the space exactly as we'd done in Istanbul, and one goal over ninety minutes was enough. That's the game in its purest form: you suffocate them on their pitch and they can't breathe.

Entry 8 worried about being caught in transition, about the gaps that open when a side comes forward on their own ground. Entry 11 had more faith. The away win proved entry 11 was closer to the truth, but not because Graz were harmless. They had their chances. They tested us. We just refused to panic, kept the shape, moved the ball through their press like they weren't there. That's not a miracle. That's what we built in Istanbul, and it traveled.

Now the European distraction ends and the real season starts. Gençlerbirliği on Saturday is the opening fixture and the kind of game where you're supposed to cruise, except you never do because your mind is still half in Graz and the domestic campaign has no mercy for teams still thinking about European nights. We'll win. We're better than they are. But the Sturm nights taught us something about consistency that the league won't care about unless we remember it when the crowd is smaller and the opposition is weaker and everything feels like it should happen on its own.

Beşiktaş play Hradec Králové tomorrow in the Europa League and I'm hoping they remember nothing about how to stay compact away from home.
