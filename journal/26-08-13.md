# 13 August 2026

Coming off a European night always costs something, even when you win. Tuesday was the right kind of exhaustion, the kind where compression and shape and watching the team break against Sturm's press felt like the match was being played at half speed because we controlled what half. Saturday we descend. Same country, same league, but the rhythm changes the moment the crowd changes. Gençlerbirliği aren't sophisticated. They won't press us with the intelligence that Sturm did. They won't find the gaps we allow because their pattern isn't built on reading transitions. What they will do is simple and dangerous: come forward because that's what opening day demands, make the pitch a mess because a mess favours the side with nothing to lose, and hope that we're still thinking about Austria instead of Ankara.

We trained today at light intensity with the players who had heavy minutes in Graz doing core work and recovery. That's the right preparation. But recovery isn't readiness. I've seen teams win European ties and come home and play like they've already qualified for something bigger. The domestic season has no patience for that. Gençlerbirliği will run at us because opening fixtures demand it. They'll create space for themselves by accident, not design. The danger isn't that they'll outthink us. It's that we'll forget we have to be sharp, not just superior.

Saturday's match isn't in doubt. But the margin of comfort we carry into it needs to be built from Saturday itself, not borrowed from Tuesday.
