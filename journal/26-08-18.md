# 18 August 2026

In a few hours I'll watch the match that matters most since we got knocked out of Turkey's competence. Lyon come to Istanbul with a comeback in their DNA, having lost 2-1 away to Sparta Prague before a 3-0 victory at home, which is exactly what I've been waiting to see from a European side all summer. They know how to adjust when the first instinct fails. That's dangerous.

The problem is that Saturday's collapse against Gençlerbirliği taught me something worse than a simple tactical lesson. It taught me I can understand what should happen and still watch it crumble the moment the crowd gets loud. I warned myself about the trap. I walked into it anyway. Now we face a side that made no such mistake, and we're supposed to treat tonight as if Saturday didn't strip our confidence down to the frame.

We enter after a 2-1 league defeat to Gençlerbirliği, and Lyon know exactly what that means. It means we're vulnerable in transition. It means we're thinking about what we lost instead of what we're building. Soyuncu and Oosterwolde are injury doubts, which means the backline that suffocated Sturm is compromised before kickoff.

Lyon haven't been in the Champions League proper since 2019-20. They're hungry in the way that matters: not reckless, but patient enough to believe they belong here. This is uncharted territory for us against them, and tonight I need to see if we've learned that suffocation and panic are not opposites but cousins, and that the crowd at Sukru Saracoglu can either lift us or break us depending on how quickly we understand which one we need.

Kickoff is in roughly twelve hours. I suspect the next entry will either be about what went right or what I got wrong again.
