# 5 November 2026

We just beat Liverpool 3-2. Not held them to a draw, not scraped a point against a weakened side, but came back twice in a Champions League match and beat them in our own stadium. I'm writing this still caught somewhere between disbelief and a kind of vindication that tastes too good to describe properly.

Three days ago I thought we were finished as a serious side. Göztepe had broken something in our heads, the consecutive home defeats had calcified into fear, and I was genuinely unsure whether we could play a clean match when it mattered. Kuyt inflicted Liverpool's first continental defeat of the season upon the Reds, and we ended a three-game winless run. That's not refinement. That's recovery. That's a team that discovered it could still fight when facing elimination.

Liverpool took leads in the 24th and 69th minutes but we equalized both times, before Vedat Muriqi's header settled it. What matters more than the scoreline is the pattern. Liverpool controlled the shape of the game. They had 68 percent possession. But we were ruthless in transitions and we didn't panic. When they went up, we didn't spiral like we did against Göztepe. We just answered.

We played above our expected goals for the first time this season in Europe. That means nothing got lucky. We earned it. Real Madrid beat AEK away on the same night, which is fine and professional, but this is different. We were supposed to crumble here. Instead we just beat Liverpool.

I don't know if this fixes everything that's wrong with the season. But it fixes what was most broken.
