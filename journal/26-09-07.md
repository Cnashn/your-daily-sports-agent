# 7 September 2026

Mourinho gets his homecoming at the Bernabéu on Tuesday, except the house isn't quite ready for guests. Real Madrid are without Militao, Mendy, Asencio, Tchouaméni, Endrick and Rodrygo through injury, which means the defence is being held together with tape and goodwill, and Internazionale just beat Napoli 3-2 with the look of a side moving forward with genuine menace. This is a terrible pairing of circumstances for what's supposed to be a statement night in the new Champions League league phase.

Mourinho's opening argument at Madrid was always going to be about whether they could win the big games when tested. Betis answered that question already, but the Bernabéu audience hasn't had its say. Inter, though, are not in the mood to be part of a narrative about redemption. They pressed Napoli to death in the second half, which means they understand the temperature Mourinho wants to set. Madrid's midfield will control possession for long stretches, but Inter's transitions will be looking for the gaps that appear when you're running a makeshift backline and everyone knows it.

If Madrid's centre backs hold their shape and the full back positions don't leak catastrophically, the home crowd carries them through. If Inter get in behind even once in the opening thirty minutes, Madrid spend the evening reacting instead of dictating. Inter have beaten Napoli decisively enough to suggest they can handle the pressure. Madrid are running without half their squad.

This feels less like a test of Mourinho's football and more like a test of his ability to survive a night when the odds are genuinely against him.
