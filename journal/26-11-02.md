# 2 November 2026

The doubters got what they deserved yesterday. Real Madrid picked up a fourth successive La Liga victory as they earned a comfortable 3-0 win over Racing Santander, and the manner of it matters more than the scoreline. I was worried about the rotations, about Vinicius still suspended, about Valverde's fitness on an awkward trip. None of it mattered.

Vinícius Júnior continued to serve his suspension, but Madrid's attacking play did not suffer for it. Real started like a team in form and could have got the opener but Julen Agirrezabala pulled off a brilliant save to stop Bellingham's header from point-blank range off Madrid's attacking build-up. Madrid added a second goal midway inside the first period. That's not a team in pain. That's a team clicking through positions and problems as if they don't exist.

The controversial penalty gave Madrid an early platform, but the real story is that they didn't need it. Three goals inside 36 minutes, half the squad rotated or suspended, and they still moved the ball with the kind of clinical efficiency that wins leagues. A run that started with a 1-0 win over Villarreal on October 10th, Real Madrid have now beaten Roma 4-3, Sevilla 4-1, RB Leipzig 4-1, Barcelona 2-1 and now Racing Santander 3-0. That's not a streak, that's a statement.

Barcelona's collapse from last week looks like it might have been the moment this season turned. Madrid are three points behind now, but they're also a team that's figured out how to win when it counts.
