# 23 September 2026

Beşiktaş lost 3-2 to Amed Sportif Faaliyetler on Sunday, which is the kind of result that ought to be embarrassing for a club that size competing in Turkey's top flight. They were at home, exposing their shape so thoroughly that the opponent scored three times. This wasn't a tactical misstep or a bad day. This was a team that has no idea how to structure a defense under pressure.

Amed play direct, aggressive football with no subtlety. They press high and they break fast, the kind of opponent that punishes soft positioning and indecision in midfield. Beşiktaş gave them everything they wanted. No compactness, no communication, no recovery shape when the ball turned over. That's not a friendly fixture surprise. That's a side showing fundamental problems that go deeper than personnel.

The real damage isn't the loss itself, it's what it says about Beşiktaş's readiness for what's coming. The Süper Lig is weeks away from real pressure, and if they're conceding three at home, Fenerbahçe and Galatasaray are already thinking about how to exploit the same gaps. Beşiktaş have the squad to compete. They've just proven they don't have the discipline or awareness to use it when it matters.

Galatasaray got hammered 4-0 by Trabzonspor just days earlier, and now Beşiktaş are leaking goals to opponents they should be controlling. One bad result is noise. Two of the biggest clubs in Turkey both getting exposed in the same week suggests something systemic is broken at the top of the league.
