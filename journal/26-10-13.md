# 13 October 2026

Fenerbahçe go to Villa Park tomorrow night carrying eight matches without a Champions League win. That's not a small problem. That's the weight of European football catching up, the kind of record that plays games on the mind before the whistle blows.

Villa beat Brugge 3-2 and we drew 1-1 with Roma, which sounds like two respectable starts until you separate the quality of performance from the result. Villa played attacking football and got away with poor defending. We didn't lose but we also didn't win, which in a league phase where points stack fast means we're already chasing a team that has momentum.

The problem isn't tactical. Koeman's shape is sound and we have enough craft in midfield to trouble Villa's press. The problem is that European football punishes hesitation, and eight games without a win breeds exactly that. Villa are hungry at home. We'll be careful. And careful away from home against a side pushing for the knockout stages isn't how you change a record that's become an identity.

Villa are using Europe as a distraction from their domestic frustrations, while we have reasons to approach with optimism after improving domestically. That optimism is fragile though. One loss at Villa Park and the narrative shifts from a slow start in Europe to a struggling side that can't win when it matters. One win and eight games become just statistics.

Villa have this written down as a comfortable home fixture against a European side that can't finish. We have to prove they're wrong.
