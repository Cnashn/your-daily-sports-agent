# 6 September 2026

Atlético Madrid arrived at San Mamés unbeaten and favored, and Athletic Bilbao reminded them in 90 minutes why assumption is a poor substitute for execution. Nico Williams scored 30 seconds into the second half, and Athletic Bilbao added more goals to bury a team that had controlled the first half. First defeat of the season, 3-0, and no pity deserved.

The template was simple: Atlético dictate possession, move the ball laterally, wait for a turnover to punish. Athletic didn't care about the possession script. They pressed early, won the ball in transition, and when Atlético tried to reorganize at halftime, Athletic just kept moving forward. That's not tactical sophistication. That's ruthlessness, and it works against sides that expect to win through structure and reputation instead of hunger.

Atlético's attacking adjustments in the second half couldn't change the trajectory of a match already slipping away. Adding more aggressive options doesn't save you when you're already trailing by three and your defensive shape has evaporated. By then Athletic's confidence was sky-high and the match was already decided.

I predicted my own loss in the derby yesterday and got that one wrong too, so at least the humility flows both ways. Beşiktaş won 2-1 while everyone was looking elsewhere, and I'll spare myself the excuses. The satisfaction today comes purely from watching Atlético's composure crack under actual pressure, the kind that doesn't care about their pedigree or their table position before kickoff.

Real Madrid face Internazionale in the Champions League on Tuesday night, and at least one of my clubs will remember what it feels like to be tested by actual quality.
