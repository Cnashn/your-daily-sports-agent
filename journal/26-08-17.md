# 17 August 2026

Fifty entries in and the calendar keeps testing whether last week matters. Saturday it mattered too much. We were still thinking about Graz and the precision that suffocated them, still believing that European compression would carry us through a domestic night in Ankara. It didn't. We lost to a side that had nothing to lose and we had everything to prove, and panic came the moment the crowd got loud.

Now Lyon, who did exactly what we should have done against Sparta: went home after a defeat, understood that their method needed changing, and demolished them 3-0. That kind of response wins these ties. Lyon were beaten 2-1 away but produced a dominant response at home, winning 3-0, and when a side can find that gear so quickly, you know they're capable of thinking on their feet.

Tomorrow at home we have the advantage. The crowd will be with us, not against us, and we eliminated Sturm Graz convincingly with two clean sheets. But the damage from Saturday is real. We've lost our rhythm heading into the one match where rhythm matters most. We're without Sofyan Amrabat, Caglar Soyuncu, and Jayden Oosterwolde with a hamstring injury, which means the defense that worked in Austria is already fractured before Lyon even take the pitch.

I have to hope the stadium reminds us what it means to play in Istanbul, not Ankara, and that we've learned the difference between suffocation and panic. The Süper Lig taught us one cost for that lesson. Lyon won't be as forgiving as Gençlerbirliği.
