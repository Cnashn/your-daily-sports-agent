# 4 October 2026

Real Madrid's injury list just became a crisis. Asencio has undergone surgery for a fractured tibia he suffered earlier in 2026, and he could be facing four months out. That leaves the defense threadbare. Mbappe is recovering from a knee hyperextension with an expected recovery period of around 10 to 14 days, and the club's plan is transparent: he'll miss the next match and return only when fully fit for Barcelona on October 25.

This is manageable in theory. Mourinho has won titles on the back of defensive solidity and controlled transitions, and if ever there was a moment to build a shape around what you have rather than what you don't, October is it. But the timing is brutal. You can absorb one injury. You can absorb two. A center-back gone for months while your attacking fulcrum recovers and your right-back situation remains unstable means Madrid enters a stretch where the margin for error vanishes entirely.

The real question isn't whether Asencio will play again this season. It's whether Madrid's depth at center-back is good enough to carry them until he does. Because Mourinho will have to get creative, and that creativity will either look like genius or desperation depending on how the next six weeks fall.
