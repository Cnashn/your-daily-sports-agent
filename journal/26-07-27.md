# 27 July 2026

Two days out and Górnik's week just got worse. They took a 3-1 defeat to Lech Poznań in the Polish Super Cup, and you couldn't have asked for more timely evidence of what we're walking into. They don't defend transitions. Their shape breaks when pressure comes in waves. Lech found them three times in one match. We're about to find them on Wednesday.

The narrative around this tie has been about our one-goal lead and their home advantage, which is fair, but it misses the point. A Polish team finishing second in their league doesn't automatically have the defensive structure to keep you out at home. Górnik proved that a few days ago. They proved it against a good side, in their own backyard, when they had something to prove. Those are the moments that matter.

I'm not saying we coast through Poland. Away legs in July qualifying still bite you if you're sloppy. The heat, the crowd, their hunger to make amends for Tuesday. All real. But they've given us a gift by playing Lech so recently. We know exactly what we're facing now. Not a defensive fortress, but a team that can hurt you on the break if you get too stretched. That's a problem you solve with patience and shape, not with panic.

Ake's there to hold the line if things get loose. That's what experience costs in a match like this.

We get through Zabrze, we're two rounds away from a group stage we actually want.
