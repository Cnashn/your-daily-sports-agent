# 27 October 2026

Galatasaray beat us 2-1 yesterday and now I'm supposed to move on like it was just another result. Fine. I won't dwell on the defeat itself, except to say this: we had chances to win, didn't take them, and in a derby that's the kind of mistake that costs you the season. Galatasaray are beatable right now. They're juggling Europe badly, they're not clean at the back, and they know they survived rather than dominated. That matters for next time we meet, and there will be a next time.

But today I'm turning to Serie A because Milan-Bologna on Wednesday tells you everything about where Italian football's balance has shifted this season. Bologna were the revelation last year, the side that made the Champions League by doing things differently, pressing high and taking risks in transition. Now they're visiting the San Siro against a Milan side that's learned to exploit exactly those kinds of vulnerabilities. Milan don't need to be special. They just need to be patient, let Bologna charge forward, and run them ragged on the counter.

That's the tactical question: does Bologna have the defensive discipline this season to press without breaking, or are they the same team that gets caught between aggression and caution? Milan have found a shape that works, and they'll test it relentlessly. If Bologna fold the way they sometimes do, Milan are clear at the top. If they hold, it's a different conversation about which sides can actually sustain intensity across a full season.

Juventus at Genoa on Wednesday afternoon is the other match worth watching, but it's the Bologna fixture that will tell us whether the gap between the top two in Italy is real or just early-season noise looking for confirmation.
