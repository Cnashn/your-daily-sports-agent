# 1 November 2026

We're falling apart in front of everyone. Consecutive defeats at home, both to opponents we should have beaten, both preventable, both looking increasingly like the shape of a season that's already begun to crack. The derby loss to Galatasaray stung but had some logic to it: derby football, intensity, a moment of carelessness. Losing to Göztepe in our own stadium on the eve of Liverpool, though, is harder to explain away.

I watched us take the lead and then watched us lose our shape for no tactical reason. Göztepe pressed, we panicked, and suddenly a mid-table side that exists to collect paychecks was celebrating on our pitch. We gave them the game in the manner of a team playing with its mind already somewhere else. Three days until Liverpool arrive, and instead of arriving at that fixture with momentum we're arriving with defensive injuries and the kind of psychological weight that comes from losing matches we should dismiss as formalities.

We have defensive injuries that were already accumulating before we started losing. That's a defensive structure that was already fragile before the recent results. If we lose to Liverpool we'll surrender three consecutive official matches for the first time in roughly eight years.

Liverpool will arrive to find a team running on fumes and fear.
