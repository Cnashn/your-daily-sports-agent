# 15 August 2026

Galatasaray drew with a newly promoted side at home yesterday and the stink of it is still fresh. They were held to a 2-2 draw by newly promoted Çorum FK at RAMS Park in a match that initially appeared to be under the home side's control but eventually turned into a real battle. Worse: they were 2-1 down and needed a brace from Victor Osimhen with one goal coming in the 90th minute just to salvage a point. Their fans chanted for management to resign after Çorum's second goal.

This is the sort of opening day that defines a season before it starts. Çorum finished the match with 10 men but survived the champions' pressure, which means Galatasaray spent the second half pressing a side down to one fewer player and still couldn't break them until desperation set in. The four-time defending champions. The direct entry into the Champions League. Unable to beat children at home.

The issue isn't the dropped points, though those sting enough. It's that this is exactly the sort of match that reveals whether the preseason dominance was real or just an August mirage. Galatasaray looked heavy, slow to react, uncertain about where the danger was coming from. Çorum played without fear or shape, just bodies and hunger, and that shouldn't have troubled a side with their pedigree. But it did. The question now is whether this was an anomaly or the shape of the season to come.

Meanwhile I've got Gençlerbirliği in a few hours and the opposite problem: we're carrying the European nights too confidently into domestic football, forgetting that the crowd wants chaos and our shape wants patience.
