# 21 October 2026

I had them beaten and couldn't hold it. That's the ugliest feeling in European football: not that you lost, but that you let a win slip through your hands in the last minute when you should have been running down the clock, protecting a one-goal lead, doing the small things that separate teams that belong in this competition from teams still learning.

We started perfectly, took the lead through Aydın's curling effort after four minutes and doubled it through Greenwood's half-volley in the 11th minute. The crowd was alive, Slavia looked dead, and we should have finished the match in the first half. Instead we retreated. We began retreating into our own half, allowing Slavia to build momentum, and produced a timid second half performance. A Guendouzi pass was ill-judged, with Provod finishing from close range before halftime, and suddenly we're defending for our lives.

This is the pattern. Fenerbahçe attack with urgency early, create space, score goals. Then we lose our nerve and invite pressure we cannot sustain. We made a vital stop to deny Nene a third goal in the second half, which means we had chances to kill the match and didn't take them. And in the 90th minute, Kacor connected strongly to a fine cross from Ayaosi to equalize.

The point stops the bleeding from 6-0 and stays us from complete collapse. But it also confirms the real problem: we cannot sustain a lead. We cannot manage a game once we're ahead. That is not a one-match issue. That is a mentality issue, and it will cost us far more before this campaign is over.

The next three matches will decide whether we stay alive in this competition at all.
