# 10 August 2026

The Hearts tie did something useful for Sturm that they won't realize until tomorrow is too late. It convinced them that their home shape works, that their transitions are lethal, that bodies on their pitch move the way they need them to. Six goals over two legs creates a kind of amnesia. You forget that Hearts weren't a test, that nothing about a 6-0 aggregate tells you what happens when you face a side that knows the geometry of compression. Sturm played at home once since February and the opposition couldn't find the ball. That's the story they'll carry into Graz tomorrow.

But we went to Istanbul knowing exactly what Sturm were trying to do, and we suffocated it. The shape held because we understood that their pressing shape only works if you panic, only if you let them dictate the tempo of your recovery. We didn't. We moved the ball through their pressure like it wasn't there. Two goals, no drama.

Graz will be different. They'll come forward because they have to, because going home down 2-0 with thirty minutes left is the moment every side believes it can find something. And when sides come forward, spaces open. But that's where I'm certain, where entry 8 worried about being caught. We know how to use those spaces now. We proved it. If we treat Graz like we treated Istanbul, if we compress the space from the whistle and don't let their record on their pitch become a weapon, we move into the play-offs and Sturm realize too late that Hearts were never the real test.

I expect we'll go through without needing drama, but Sturm believing they can steal something at home is exactly the kind of false hope that makes young sides careless.
