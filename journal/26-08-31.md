# 31 August 2026

Napoli lost 1-2 to Como yesterday and the shape of this season just shifted. Not because Como are suddenly competitive, but because Napoli played like a team that forgot how to organize without Kvaratskhelia. A winger who left in January and had an entire summer for his absence to calcify into a tactical problem. Como aren't a side that dismantles organized defenses through brilliance. They press, they transition, they punish the moments when shape breaks. If Napoli can't defend the transition without Kvaratskhelia, they've built their system on someone who isn't there anymore.

That's not a chemistry problem or a quality gap. That's a fundamental architectural failure. A team should have adjusted to losing one player by now, six months later. Instead they looked like they were still waiting for him to carry the ball up the left. They conceded twice to Como. Como. A newly promoted side with a budget that wouldn't cover half of Napoli's midfield.

Napoli won Serie A three seasons ago by suffocating space and recovering the ball in the middle third. If that version of the team can't beat Como, the one wearing the scudetto from the 2022-23 season never actually existed. What we're watching is what happens when a squad gets older and a key player leaves and nobody really plugs the gap. Injuries will get worse. Form will crack. By October, if this is how they defend transitions, they'll be eight points off the pace.

The problem isn't Como. The problem is that Napoli already know it.
