# 9 October 2026

Mourinho gets three players back for Villarreal, which solves the short-term roster crisis but creates a different problem entirely. Mbappé, Konaté and Valverde are expected to be available, which means Saturday's centerback pairing doesn't have to be improvised around a 33-year-old and a midfielder. That's relief. But it's not a cure.

The real issue is that Madrid haven't kept a clean sheet in five matches, while Villarreal have not secured a shutout in all eight competitive matches this season. You don't suddenly fix a defensive pattern by getting healthy players back if the pattern itself is broken. Madrid's shape has been reactive under pressure, fullbacks dragged wide, midfield compression creating gaps rather than sealing them. Villarreal, meanwhile, have been scoring but leaking at both ends because their new coach Iñigo Pérez is still working out how to balance aggression with structure.

This is a match where Mourinho will be tempted to believe his injury problems are solved and his tactical ones can wait. They cannot. Villarreal are dangerous precisely because they play without fear now, and Madrid's returning players won't automatically recreate compactness. If anything, availability might breed overconfidence at a moment when the defensive fundamentals still need rebuilding.

Konaté's return is the key. A functioning center-back pairing changes whether Mourinho can build narrow or has to stay open, and narrow is where Madrid's fullbacks stop getting murdered.
