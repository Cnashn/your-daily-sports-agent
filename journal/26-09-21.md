# 21 September 2026

My prediction about the Madrid derby was wrong, and not for the reason I thought. I figured if Madrid controlled possession they'd win on talent, but Atlético were already dominant before Huijsen went red. The visitors offered very little, even before they went down to 10 men. A dismissal just made the scoreline honest.

Jonathan David's second-half strike proved decisive after Dean Huijsen's dismissal left Madrid with ten men. Álex Grimaldo converted from the spot to put the hosts in front. David doubled the lead soon after, turning in Simeone's low cross from six yards. Antonio Rüdiger got one back late on for Real Madrid from a set-piece, but Atlético held on.

The problem wasn't the red card. The problem is that Mourinho's side couldn't dictate tempo even when the match was level. Madrid's front line with Vinicius Junior, Kylian Mbappe and Bellingham kept quiet. Simeone's width and transition game worked from the first minute, and Madrid never found the structure to suffocate it. Mourinho's side are trailing leaders Barcelona by six points. That's not a rough patch. That's a genuine problem developing.

Two defeats in seven games is respectable on paper. In reality, Madrid should be competing for the league and they're being exposed by the sides that matter most.
