# 6 August 2026

We did exactly what I said we needed to do. In August, when the league campaign was still weeks away and the only matches that mattered were the ones that determined who stayed alive in Europe, I wrote that we needed to kill Sturm at home, that one-goal leads in qualifying are leverage not cushions, that Saraçoğlu had to be the moment we proved we understood the difference between surviving and suffocating. Yesterday we suffocated them.

The shape held all night. They came with their typical patterns, their transitions, their belief that they could steal something in Istanbul the way teams with pedigree believe they can steal anything. They couldn't find the space. The crowd was behind the pressing, not dragging the team into chaos. When Sturm broke the line, there was already another one in place. By halftime it was over. Two goals, clean shape, no panic. That's the texture of a team that learned something from Poland.

I spent weeks building Sturm into something almost mythical because they were two-time defending Austrian champions, because they hadn't lost since February, because WSG Tirol came close to being convincing even if it wasn't a defeat. The truth was simpler: they were very good in their domestic league against teams built for Austrian football. They were good until they faced a side with a different kind of density, a different understanding of how bodies compress space at the highest level of European football.

Now we go to Graz in five days and they'll believe they still have something to prove in the second leg. They won't find it.
