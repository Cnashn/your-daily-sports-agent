# 25 July 2026

Quiet mornings are when you remember the small moments that shaped things. Exactly twenty years ago, July 2006, there was a story that barely registered at the time but speaks volumes in hindsight. Turkey's football federation was in the shadow of the World Cup that had just finished in Germany, where we crashed out in the group stage despite a decent showing. Nobody was thinking about that anymore. Fenerbahçe were moving, Real Madrid were consolidating, and the rest of us were trying to forget another summer of what might have been.

Now I'm sitting here in 2026 watching Fenerbahçe move upward at exactly the speed that matters, one qualified round at a time, one smart signing at a time. Ake came in. Garcia's situation got complicated in ways the Istanbul clubs can't control anymore. We've got a one-goal lead in Poland waiting for us in a week. Real Madrid are still trying to convince themselves that three new defenders fixes a decade of creeping decline.

Twenty years is a long time to wait for a pattern to make sense. You watch Turkish football long enough and you start to see the rhythm. The clubs that matter don't panic. They build. They move for players when it matters, not when the market screams at them to.

Galatasaray lost two nil to Monza yesterday in a friendly that matters to nobody, which is exactly the kind of game you want to lose when there's still time to fix things before it counts.
