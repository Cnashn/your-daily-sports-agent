# 10 October 2026

Rizespor at 16:00 UTC is six hours away, and I haven't thought much about them because the Süper Lig doesn't demand advance study the way European football does. But that's the danger with Fenerbahçe away matches in the league: the illusion of control. Rizespor are not a threat. They're a fixture. And fixtures are where you drop points because you showed up half-present.

The Turkish league's rhythm is strange for this time of year. The season starts in August and runs without international breaks like a continental competitor, which means by October the squad looks soft if the mentality has slipped. One match, three points, move on. That's not a philosophy, that's a result-counting exercise. Fenerbahçe have the quality to beat Rizespor, which is precisely why they might not.

Koeman's first real test under fixture congestion came last weekend. Today is the second. If this team is serious about defending a title or pushing for Europe, these matches have to be automatic. The moment away games feel manageable is the moment the title slips.

I'm not worried. I'm just waiting to see if they actually know what's at stake when the opponent is small enough to ignore.
