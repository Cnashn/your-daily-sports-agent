# 30 July 2026

I watched us draw 1-1 away at Górnik and advance 2-1 on aggregate, which is the definition of getting through when you needed to but nothing more. We were supposed to go to Poland, defend a one-goal lead with shape and discipline, and suffocate them. Instead we conceded and spent the second half hoping the scoreline held. It did, but that's survival, not control.

The first leg at home felt clean. Quiet. You beat a Polish side 1-0 and you think you've learned how to play them. Then the away fixture teaches you that a one-goal lead is exactly the kind of margin that makes you nervous, and nervous teams get stretched into the spaces where they actually know what they're doing. That's what happened. They pressed, found gaps, equalized. We held on.

We're through to the next round because 2-1 on aggregate keeps us moving forward. But I came into this week thinking we'd be the side that went to Poland and killed it off, and instead we're the side that went to Poland and scraped through with a draw. That's not the trajectory I wanted. Not yet anyway.

Real Madrid had a friendly somewhere mid-preseason, probably won it, probably rotated. Doesn't matter today.

Two rounds from a league phase that actually means something, and we just learned that home and away tell very different stories about who we are.
