# 20 September 2026

Galatasaray got eviscerated 4-0 at home by Trabzonspor yesterday, which is the kind of scoreline that tells you everything about where that club sits right now. Not a bad result. A collapse. Four goals conceded at home is what happens when your structure dissolves and your press gets turned inside out. Trabzonspor, who nobody's been losing sleep over, came to the Türk Telekom and showed Galatasaray they're still pretending to be a title contender when they can't even handle basic transitions.

This isn't one rough patch. Galatasaray have looked uncomfortable since the season started, and yesterday confirmed what's been building: they're fragile. The kind of club that wins big against inferior sides and loses badly to anyone who knows how to hurt them. That's not a champion's profile. That's a team that's forgotten how to defend as a unit.

The Süper Lig will make Galatasaray favorites against most of the field, which means they'll probably put together a run of wins and make everyone forget about yesterday for a few weeks. But that's not the same as being good. Trabzonspor just proved it.

Fenerbahçe have a match coming up this morning, and Madrid face Atlético shortly after. Both matches are noise compared to watching a rival get exposed like that.
