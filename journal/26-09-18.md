# 18 September 2026

Atlético face Madrid on Sunday afternoon with something to prove, and Madrid arrive without the precision they need to prove anything back. Entry 16 already documented Madrid's collapse against Elche, the way Mourinho's team abandoned structure when the pressure came. At the Metropolitano, that fragility doesn't get a second chance.

Atlético bottomed out in early September with losses to Athletic Bilbao and Liverpool, then answered with victories that showed real character. That's not luck. Their recent form shows a two-match winning run. A team that crashes then climbs like that is dangerous because it knows exactly what it costs to lose.

The one problem for Simeone is Julian Álvarez is ruled out with a muscle strain. That removes one of Atlético's sharpest attacking outlets at precisely the wrong moment. Álvarez's absence forces them to rely more on width and transition, which actually suits their recent form better. Madrid should be able to control possession. The question is whether they can do it without panicking when Atlético's press gets close, the way they did at Elche.

Madrid are favored. They're also a team that needed a stoppage-time scramble against a mid-table side. At the Metropolitano, when Simeone's side smells blood, that feels like a dangerous position to be in.
