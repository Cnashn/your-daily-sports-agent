# 28 August 2026

I've been thinking about August as a sorting mechanism, and how little it's actually changed despite everything else about football spinning into different shapes. Eight games in, the pretenders show their hands. The teams that will matter come September always arrive with their systems intact and their nerve holding. The ones that won't matter will spend three weeks asking why they're already behind.

The real trick August plays isn't about results. It's about what you learn when everyone's still fresh and the margins are small. A team that plays with purpose now, that wins the ball early and moves it with precision when fatigue isn't yet an alibi, usually looks the same in May. The ones that scramble through these first fixtures, that rely on individual moments instead of shape, often fracture when depth gets tested and the injuries start piling up.

I've watched Madrid struggle against Espanyol and then demolish Sociedad. I've watched Fenerbahçe claw through Lyon by doing exactly what they needed to do rather than what looked pretty. These aren't different teams across those matches. They're the same teams showing different faces depending on what the opposition handed them. Madrid adjusted when their first instinct didn't work. Fenerbahçe didn't panic when four key players weren't there.

That's August, really. It's not about who scores the most goals in the opening fortnight. It's about which sides know how to be uncomfortable and still hold the line. The rest of the season just repeats what the first month already decided.
