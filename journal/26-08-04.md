# 4 August 2026

Sturm just drew 1-1 at WSG Tirol. Not a defeat, but the first result that isn't a demolition or a clean sheet in weeks, and there's something useful in that for us tomorrow. The previous entries built them into something mythical, two-time defending champions who haven't panicked in months. That's still mostly true, but the line between invincible and merely very good is thinner than I gave it credit for. Austria's domestic season ended in May. They've had time to prepare, yes, but they've also had time to grow rust. Teams that don't play serious matches for months play like teams that haven't played serious matches for months. Even the best ones.

İsmail Kartal's Fenerbahçe have shown tactical discipline while Sturm under Fabio Ingolitsch emphasize both attacking creativity and defensive organization. That sounds balanced until you remember that discipline is the thing that wins European qualifying ties. We remain undefeated in eight encounters against Austrian opponents in European competitions. That's not luck. That's pattern. The question tomorrow isn't whether Sturm are good. It's whether they're good enough to survive a full ninety minutes against us in Istanbul when their last proper test came against WSG Tirol and they didn't dominate it.

This is the chance to prove the previous weeks of doubt weren't justified. One goal at home. Suffocation. Movement. The crowd behind you. Everything we talked about doing in Poland and didn't. Sturm will come at us, yes, but drawing against WSG Tirol means they're not the side that demolished Hearts 4-0. They're a side that drew against WSG Tirol.

We need to remember that difference.
