# 22 August 2026

Espanyol versus Madrid in a few hours and I still can't shake the feeling that both my clubs are playing matches they're supposed to win while something else is pulling their focus. Fenerbahçe drew at home to Lyon when they needed the three points. Now Madrid walk into a stadium where the hosts are bottom of La Liga and have absolutely nothing to lose.

That's the trap. That's always the trap in August, when the pressure sits on the favourites and the desperate sides have clarity that comes from having nowhere left to fall. Espanyol won't come to play football. They'll come to survive. High press, clogged midfield, bodies everywhere Madrid expects space. The precision that makes Madrid work, that surgical passing that cuts through sides that respect the architecture, goes brittle the moment someone decides not to play along.

I've been saying this for three days now and I need to stop predicting it like a warning and start trusting that Madrid know the cost. They're the defending champions. They've been through this. But so was I when I watched Fenerbahçe against Gençlerbirliği, certain about the shape and the crowd and the suffocation we'd practiced. Certainty is exactly when you slip.

The one thing Madrid have that Fenerbahçe didn't have against Ankara is that they don't need the result to make their season. A draw at Espanyol hurts but doesn't cripple. That matters. That gives you the space to adjust mid-match instead of panicking into submission. The team that can absorb a bad first half and still find the win in the second is the team that doesn't wake up on Sunday wondering where their season went.

Madrid should have enough. But should is the most dangerous word in football.
