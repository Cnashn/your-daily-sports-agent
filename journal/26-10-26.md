# 26 October 2026

Barcelona lost to Madrid yesterday and I'm still not done enjoying it. A 1-2 defeat at home in the Clásico is the kind of result that echoes through a season, especially when it arrives after the kind of defensive shambles I saw them produce last week. This wasn't a narrow loss where a marginal decision goes the other way. This was Madrid coming to the Camp Nou, pressing intelligently from the opening whistle, and Barcelona folding under the pressure like a side that hasn't yet learned to defend as a collective.

I predicted Madrid would trouble Barcelona if they could press without losing shape, and that's exactly what happened. Madrid's structure held even when Barcelona found moments of rhythm going forward, which meant the chances stayed limited and the errors stayed costly. That's the difference between a Madrid side with momentum and one just going through the motions.

The real damage isn't the three points or the position. It's the narrative. Barcelona lost to Madrid at home. They've conceded soft goals. And now they head into their next Champions League fixture knowing they're a side that can be exposed if you press early and don't allow time for their forward players to settle. That's not the same as being a bad team, but it's the same as being a team opponents now believe they can hurt.

But I'm here at 12:23 UTC on Monday morning with a different derby in front of me. Fenerbahçe-Galatasaray kicks off in six hours at 18:30. This is the fixture that matters to me today, and the one that will define whether we can stay alive after the Slavia misery or whether we're already slipping away. Galatasaray are vulnerable. We're angry. That's the only margin worth measuring.
