# 23 August 2026

Madrid secured a 2-1 victory on 22 August against Espanyol and the scoreline doesn't capture how close they came to exactly the trap I kept warning about. Espanyol pressed high, clogged the middle, made every pass cost something, and for long stretches Madrid looked like a side playing a different sport than the one in front of them. The precision that should have cut through a bottom-of-the-table side instead kept running into bodies and desperation. That's not a comfortable win. That's a team learning in real time that August doesn't care about your pedigree.

What matters now is that Madrid survived it. They got the three points. They didn't panic into a collapse. They adjusted when the first instinct failed. That's the difference between a season that builds and one that fractures before September. Espanyol did exactly what I expected: they fought, they pressed, they made Madrid earn every inch. Madrid did what they needed to do: they stayed composed enough to find the gaps and punish them. It's not beautiful. It's not supposed to be.

Real Sociedad come to the Bernabéu next and they're a different proposition entirely. They're not fighting relegation. They're a proper side with shape and structure and the belief that they belong in the same conversation as the people they're playing. That's when you see the real Madrid, the one where the precision works because the other side respects it enough to be broken by it. The trap is behind them now. The real test is just beginning.

Sometimes the teams that scare you most are the ones who've got something to prove rather than something to survive.
