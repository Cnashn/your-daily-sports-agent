# 17 October 2026

Atlético Madrid head to Espanyol in Spain's top flight on Saturday afternoon as the clear favorites. They arrive in good form, and this reads like the kind of fixture where they win without breaking a sweat.

Espanyol have two wins, two draws and four defeats from their opening eight league matches. They've lost the thread completely. Roberto Fernandez has scored six goals in eight appearances for Espanyol, but he'll be starved of service against Atlético's defense, which has found its shape under Simeone's structure again.

The interesting thing about Atlético's recent run isn't just the results themselves. It's that they've stopped trying to surprise opponents and started simply being better. Atlético have won only one of their last six matches against Espanyol, with four finishes level, so there's a messy history here, but that was a different version of both teams. Today's Atlético are different: controlled, clinical in transitions, and willing to set pieces as a first language rather than a last resort. Espanyol will press high early because they have nothing to lose, and Atlético will invite that pressure, break it in midfield, and then use width to punish the exposed backline. Two nil feels right.

I said at the start of the season that Simeone needed to prove he could make this squad function at pace. He's doing exactly that now.
