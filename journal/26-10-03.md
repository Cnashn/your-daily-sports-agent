# 3 October 2026

Fenerbahçe and Turan Tovuz last met thirty-two years ago in the 1994 UEFA Cup, when we won both legs convincingly. Now we're meeting again in a friendly in Baku today, which means I get to think about what football was then versus what it is now, and how much the game changes while the basics never do.

The revenue from today's match goes to the Karabakh Revival Fund, which matters more than the scoreline will. But the scoreline will matter to me because Fenerbahçe against a mid-table Azerbaijani side in October is exactly when the squad's balance reveals itself. A preseason friendly is about fitness. An October friendly is about sharpness, about whether the transitions work, whether the pressing has teeth, whether the shape holds when the intensity goes up unexpectedly.

Turan Tovuz will not be technically brilliant. They will be direct and energetic. If we're not switched on, if the centerbacks step too high without cover, if the fullbacks get caught too far forward, the spaces open up fast. Azerbaijan Premier League football is underrated as a test. The teams may not have the individual quality of the major leagues, but the tempo is honest and the mistakes get punished immediately.

I want to see Fenerbahçe move the ball with purpose and not dwell. I want to see the pressing trigger early, the transitions vertical when they need to be, the fullbacks overlapping with timing rather than just wandering forward. A friendly is only meaningful if you actually play it like something matters. Watch for that today.
