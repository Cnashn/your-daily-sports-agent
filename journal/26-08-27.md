# 27 August 2026

Both my clubs cleared the path to where they belong, and the gap between a smooth passage and a desperate scramble is everything in August.

Fenerbahçe beat Lyon 2-1 to win 3-2 on aggregate, and I spent five days convinced they'd hold on through discipline and fear. Instead they went to Lyon and attacked. That's not the story I wrote on the eve of the second leg. The championship has never been won by a side that protected what they had in Istanbul when they could have taken everything in France. They didn't panic into a collapse. They didn't park eight players and pray. They struck twice and suffocated the response. That's the difference between a squad that squeezed through and one that announced itself.

Real Madrid demolished Real Sociedad 4-1 on the road and sent a different kind of message. Sociedad are not a bottom-tier side playing for oxygen. They're a proper club with structure and belief. Madrid's precision cut through them like they weren't there. This is the Madrid that's supposed to dominate La Liga, not the one I watched struggle against Espanyol two weeks ago. The trajectory matters more than the scoreline. Wins in August are only building blocks. Wins that look like that are the ones that carry through to May.

Both sides are through to their competitions. Both sides showed what they actually are when the pressure arrived. Now Samsunspor come to Fenerbahçe on Sunday and Málaga come to the Bernabéu the same day, and a routine domestic fixture is the only thing standing between right now and September, when real football starts.

Sometimes you learn more from how a team wins than from whether they do.
