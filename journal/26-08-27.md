# 27 August 2026

They got through. After five days of writing about injuries and nerve and whether the team climbing back from nowhere had the actual spine to deliver in Lyon, Fenerbahçe got through. Three-two on aggregate, and the gates of the Champions League opened again after eighteen years.

I spent the last entry convinced they'd either hold on or collapse, as if those were the only two outcomes available. Instead they attacked. They struck twice to expose Lyon's feeble full-backs in the first half and suffocated the response from there. That's not the nervous, attrition-soaked path I'd convinced myself was their only way through. That's a team that looked at what they had available and decided to make it count.

It matters because execution under pressure is the line between a season that builds and one that fractures. Fenerbahçe could have gone to Lyon and protected what they had in Istanbul. Instead they came to win. That's not a small thing for a club that hasn't played Champions League football in nearly two decades. Lyon will have to settle for Europa League football, which is where Fenerbahçe might have ended up if their composure cracked.

Now they're in the league phase with Samsunspor on Sunday and a real European campaign ahead. The injury list is still real. The depth is still thinner than it should be. But they proved in eighty minutes in Lyon that having less doesn't mean you don't belong. Sometimes it just means you stop worrying about what you're missing and use what's there.
