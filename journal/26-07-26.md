# 26 July 2026

Three days until Poland. The gap between legs is the worst part of qualifying. You play Tuesday, you win, you think you're through, then you have to wait seventy-two hours knowing that everything can still collapse. One goal is a good position but it's also exactly the margin where away teams cause problems. We beat them 1-0 at home with a quiet performance in the first half, nothing that screamed dominance. Górnik kept their shape, tested us on the break, stayed in the match.

Now we go to them. Same pitch, different pressure. They've got nothing to lose and everything to prove. That's when a Polish side in their stadium becomes genuinely dangerous. They don't need to do much. A draw would send the match to extra time and potentially penalties. That changes the entire dynamic.

I'm not worried yet, but I'm alert. Two legs away from a league phase you actually want to reach, and you don't coast through this kind of thing. The heat will be worse in Zabrze than it was in Istanbul, the crowd will push harder, and we'll be expected to kill it off while they've had five days to think about what went wrong at home and how to fix it. That's the nature of two-legged ties in July. You play the away leg second, which means you play it against a team with a revenge angle and nothing left to lose.

Ake's debut will help. Experience matters when the walls close in.
