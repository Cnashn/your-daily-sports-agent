# 3 November 2026

There's nothing to do now but wait for Liverpool. Two home defeats in four days, both to sides we should have dispatched without thought, and now we arrive at the biggest fixture our season will face carrying the kind of psychological weight that makes everything harder. The Göztepe loss especially stings because it wasn't heroic or complicated. We took the lead, lost our shape for no tactical reason, panicked, and handed them the game. That's not football. That's a team playing with its attention somewhere else entirely.

The defensive injuries are real and they matter, but they were already there before we started losing. We didn't suddenly break defensively in 48 hours. We broke in our heads first, then our shape followed. Liverpool don't care about excuses. They arrive to find a side running on fumes and self-doubt, and they'll smell that immediately.

Beşiktaş drew with Kasımpaşa on Sunday, which is exactly the kind of result that tells you nothing except that they're also stumbling in the Süper Lig, falling apart in Europe, and managing multiple crises. At least I can take one cold comfort in knowing our rivals are burning too.

Tomorrow night at 17:45 we find out whether we're a real side or just a team that looked good for three weeks.
