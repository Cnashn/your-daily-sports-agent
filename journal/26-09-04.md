# 4 September 2026

Real Madrid travel to the Benito Villamarín today knowing that Betis are one of the few sides in La Liga who actually enjoy making you uncomfortable. They press high, they recover the ball quickly in the middle third, and they're comfortable playing scrappy. Real Madrid will have ninety percent of the possession if they want it. Betis don't care. They move forward in five touches, find the seams, and leave gaps behind. The setup is almost designed to punish the moment your central midfielders lose concentration.

This is the match where we find out whether Madrid's early season form is genuine or whether they're just rolling through the soft fixtures. Ancelotti won't have Vinícius Jr for the whole match—Betis know that, and they'll target exhaustion on the left in the second half. If Madrid can control the press before it gets dangerous, keep possession of the ball in the middle third for seventy minutes, and finish the first chance they create, they'll win comfortably. If they get sloppy with the ball or start chasing Betis's transitions, Betis will nick this, and by October Madrid will be three points adrift of whoever's running the table in Spain.

Betis have enough going on early to make this interesting, but Madrid's quality should tell eventually.
