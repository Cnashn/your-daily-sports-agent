# 8 September 2026

Mourinho walks into the Bernabéu tonight needing to answer a question that Betis already posed and Madrid flunked. The team that dominated possession and created almost nothing against a side that pressed and nicked a goal has to find a new rhythm against Inter, except the roster has gotten worse since Saturday, not better. This isn't just injuries piling on. Camavinga and Güler are both banned, which means the midfield doesn't just have bodies missing, it has specific types of body missing. Tchouaméni is highly unlikely to feature. Bellingham and Valverde are the anchors left standing, and neither is a shield.

Inter come in having beaten Napoli 3-2 and looking like they remember what urgency feels like. That's a threat. But Federico Dimarco is unavailable due to a knee injury, and Carlos Augusto is expected to start at left wing-back in his absence. A midfielder playing as a wing-back against Vinícius Jr is a specific kind of vulnerability, and Mourinho's tactical brain will absolutely see it. If Madrid can exploit that flank early, control the middle third through sheer technical superiority in possession, they turn this into a chess match they can win. If Bellingham gets isolated, if the transitions get too quick, Inter score.

The Bernabéu crowd carries weight tonight. Mourinho knows it. Inter are coming to prove last season's European disappointment was an outlier, but they're doing it in a stadium that expects to win every match. Madrid's depth is shot, but they're at home, and Inter's left flank just got thinner.

This is where Mourinho reminds people why he came back.
