# 2 August 2026

Three days until Sturm Graz and I'm trying not to let the Górnik second leg poison the well. We scraped through in Poland when we should have controlled it, and now the Austrian champions are coming to Saraçoğlu with something to prove and a reputation built on back-to-back titles. Sturm Graz are the two-time defending champions, which means they're not a side that panics or folds when pressure starts. They break cleanly on transitions and they don't panic when the ball comes at them. Sound familiar? Górnik taught us that one-goal leads create nervousness, and nervous teams get stretched into the spaces other teams know how to use.

Sturm have been here before. They know what Champions League qualifying feels like. That's the difference between them and a Polish side that's hungry but raw. When you face a team with European pedigree, the psychology is different. They're less likely to get into fights they can't win, more likely to keep things tight and look for the moment to strike. That's the harder thing to beat because it doesn't give you the obvious win you're looking for. It gives you a long night of suffocation.

We get them at home first. That's the chance to do what we failed to do at Górnik: go out, find control early, and kill it before the second leg becomes anything other than a formality. Wednesday is where that happens or doesn't, and if it doesn't, we'll spend August 11 in Austria learning the same lesson all over again.
