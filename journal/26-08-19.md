# 19 August 2026

The draw against Lyon stings more than it should. We played at home, had the crowd, had the shape we've been building since Austria, and somehow emerged with 1-1 when we needed the win. A knockout playoff in the Champions League isn't where you take a point and call it progress. A point here is a reprieve disguised as a result, and now we go to France needing what we should have taken in Istanbul.

I got the tactical setup right. I understood what Lyon were bringing, understood their adjustment capability from the Sparta Prague tie. What I didn't account for was how the weight of Saturday's collapse would still be in the building even when the crowd was finally on our side. We played cautious when we needed to be ruthless. We got ahead and then managed it instead of killing the tie. That's not European football at this level. That's hoping someone else makes the bigger mistake.

The second leg in Lyon is now a proper elimination match, not a formality. They come to us having held us to a draw at home, which means they'll go back to France believing they can finish this. We'll have to believe we can finish it there instead, which is an entirely different proposition. Away from Sukru Saracoglu, without the crowd, against a side that's already proven they can flip a tie on its head.

Konyaspor waits on Saturday in the league, but my mind is already in France, calculating whether a squad that panicked against Ankara can find composure in Lyon.
