# 28 October 2026

Bayern-Dortmund arrives Saturday with a scoreline already written into the rivalry. Bayern won 2-1 in the Franz Beckenbauer Supercup on 22 August 2026, so this is not a neutral contest but a rematch where one side carries momentum and the other carries a grudge. That changes the texture of Der Klassiker in ways league points alone don't capture.

Bayern defend high and can leave space behind, so quick transitions should be Dortmund's best route to goal. That's not a weakness hidden in statistics; that's a structural choice Kompany makes because his attack is suffocating enough to justify the risk. The problem for Dortmund is execution. Aleksandar Pavlović accumulated more touches than anyone else and became the principal driver of Bayern's circulation, repeatedly demanding possession centrally and moving the ball forward, which means Bayern don't just press high, they construct their midfielder into the foundation that allows it. That takes time to unhitch.

Michael Olise scored Bayern's second goal in the Supercup and his ability to move inside from wide positions, create chances, and combine around the box could again make Bayern's right side an important attacking route. If Olise runs this flank again, Dortmund's left side becomes the morning's dominant tactical story: not who survives it, but whether anyone can actually disrupt it.

Bayern are favorites because they've already beaten Dortmund in this fixture, because their press is refined, and because Dortmund still look unsettled on the ball under pressure. The gap isn't in talent. It's in timing, and that favors the side that's already had a practice run at exactly this.
