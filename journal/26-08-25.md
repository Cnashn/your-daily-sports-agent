# 25 August 2026

The second leg in Lyon is tomorrow and Fenerbahçe are playing without two of their three starting defenders, their primary goalkeeper, and the striker they brought in to change everything. Amrabat, Soyuncu and Lukaku miss the match. That's not a detail. That's half the team that was supposed to do something serious in Europe.

The first leg in Istanbul finished level and that drew came with all the frustration I wrote about a week ago. A side hungry for the Champions League for the first time in eighteen years couldn't find the margin at home. Now they're in Lyon needing an away goal or a clean sheet or just to not lose, and they're short on the exact bodies that were supposed to make this different.

This is what happens when you're a club climbing back from nowhere. You don't get to be at full strength in the moment that matters. You get to be short four or five key players and still have to find a way through because there is no next year, no backup plan, no second chance in a playoff. Win or go home. And home, for Fenerbahçe, means the Europa League if they're lucky, and a season of asking what could have been if they're not.

Lyon came here with the same hunger but less patience. Now they're at home with a draw and that's either comfort or poison depending on how they treat it. The pattern usually goes one way in these ties. One team decides to attack, the other team folds. I've been waiting since entry twenty to know which side Fenerbahçe actually are.

Tomorrow night will tell me everything.
