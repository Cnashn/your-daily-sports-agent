# 5 August 2026

Galatasaray led 1-0 at home against Rennes and walked into a second-half demolition that would have killed a season if this were August proper. They found themselves trailing 1-3 after a dramatic second-half collapse, saved only by Baris Alper Yilmaz converting a stoppage-time penalty. In a friendly it's theater. In September it's a warning sign you cannot afford to ignore.

The shape gave way. Rennes produced more total shots and several decisive moments in front of goal while Galatasaray's midfield lost the thread between defense and attack. Possession means nothing when bodies stop moving in sync. When the crowd pushes for a second goal after you've had control, when the other side smells momentum, that's when distance between your lines opens up like a chasm. Rennes found it and they exploited it without mercy.

What looked like dominant football in the first half became suffocation in the second because Galatasaray's pressing shape collapsed the moment Rennes stopped panicking. No geometry. No compression. Just scrambling and then a penalty in the 90th minute to mask the fact that the side that started with the aggression got picked apart when their opponent actually played football back.

If Rennes, a Ligue 1 side managing preseason form, can do that much damage by third gear, proper European sides will not need fourth.
