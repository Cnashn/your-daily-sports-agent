# 26 August 2026

Fenerbahçe are going into Lyon with four key players missing and a 1-1 draw that feels like both the worst and best result they could have hoped for. A draw at home was supposed to be a springboard. Instead it's a rope around the ankles going into a stadium where Lyon have shown they can turn matches around in ninety minutes.

That's the pattern in these ties. One team decides to attack, the other folds. I've been waiting since entry twenty to know which Fenerbahçe actually are, and tonight decides it. They're without their goalkeeping first choice, their best central defenders, their new striker. That's not depth. That's attrition dressed up as bad luck.

What they have is that draw in Istanbul. Away goals don't matter anymore in European football but the psychology does. They didn't lose. They didn't panic. Lyon scored first, Fenerbahçe equalized, and Fenerbahçe held the line. That's the foundation to build on, assuming they can find the composure to not chase the match in Lyon's stadium where everything they do will be five yards slower than it needs to be.

Lyon will press. Lyon will smell the injuries and the weakness. Fenerbahçe's only path through is exactly the one that got them here in the first place: discipline, efficiency, then survival. They need to not lose. They don't need to win.

Discipline has never been the thing that breaks Fenerbahçe. Nerve usually is.
