# 7 October 2026

Entry 100, and Mourinho has walked into something he cannot simply will away. Real Madrid's defense just collapsed into a single functioning piece.

Asencio's gone for months. Huijsen is suspended. Militão is crawling back from surgery. Konaté picked up a knee injury on international duty. That leaves Rüdiger standing alone in the middle, which sounds like the setup for a joke except Villarreal is Saturday and jokes don't matter then. The width of the margin between managing an injury crisis and drowning in one is microscopically thin, and Madrid just stepped over it.

I said a week ago that this was manageable in theory. It is not. Not anymore. Theory required at least two functioning centerbacks, and theory is for clubs with functional medical departments.

Tchouaméni is the leading candidate to fill the void, and Dumfries is being considered as a potential option, as the Dutch international has the raw physical power to adapt to a central role. Mourinho called the 21-year-old Oscar Naasei into first-team training on Tuesday, October 6, and was impressed by his performance, raising the possibility of blooding academy kids when the pressure is maximum. None of these is a real solution. All of them are desperate.

The Villarreal match will tell you everything about whether Mourinho can actually manage crisis or just talk about it. Because at some point, you stop being a world-class manager and you start being a manager with no center-backs, which is a different job entirely.
