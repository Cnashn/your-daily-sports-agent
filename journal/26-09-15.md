# 15 September 2026

A 0-0 away at Gaziantep after drawing 1-1 with Roma in midweek feels like the fixture tax playing out exactly as predicted. Fenerbahçe had the Champions League noise in their head, which means the Süper Lig gets the version of them that's already half-tired. That's not an excuse. That's just how this works when you're learning to carry two competitions at once.

Gaziantep aren't obstacles that demand perfection, so a goalless draw there reads as a team that didn't have the sharpness to break them open. Not a disaster. Not a point lost you should rage about. But it's the first sign that balancing Europe with domestic football requires energy management İsmail Kartal hasn't yet mastered. Roma at home in four days means he can't afford many more nights where Fenerbahçe turn up and look like they're playing at half throttle.

The Champions League league phase is eight matches scattered across the autumn. The Süper Lig is thirty-four. You don't win anything by neglecting the domestic season, but you also don't win anything by treating it as the priority once Europe starts. Fenerbahçe have to find the middle ground quickly, because Gaziantep taught them yesterday that hoping the other team will break themselves open doesn't work against sides that also know how to sit deep.

Real Madrid faces Elche tonight, and I'd expect them to be sharper than Fenerbahçe were, but the same mathematics apply to every club that plays in Europe now.
