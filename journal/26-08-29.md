# 29 August 2026

Beşiktaş got knocked out of the Europa League by Kauno Žalgiris. A Lithuanian side. Let that breathe for a moment. Not a famous name, not a club with European pedigree, not even a team that should have registered as a threat to a side that finished fourth in a top-five league last season. And yet here we are, with Beşiktaş packing their bags after a 1-0 defeat in a playoff that they were supposed to navigate on autopilot.

This is what happens when a club decides August is just filler. You go through the motions, you assume Europa League football will arrive because it always does, and then a team that has nothing to lose and no reputation to protect shows up and reminds you that belief matters more than pedigree in a single-elimination tie. Žalgiris didn't have to be brilliant. They just had to want it more than Beşiktaş could muster after a summer of complacency.

I'm not going to pretend this is a shock in the sense that it shouldn't have happened. It absolutely should have. But it's the sharpness of it that lands: a Turkish club that had Europa League football as their prize, that expected to treat this playoff as a formality, is now heading home before September. Galatasaray at least lost to proper sides in Europe this year. Beşiktaş lost to Lithuania and are paying for it with their entire autumn.

The Süper Lig just got smaller.
