# 28 July 2026

Just over thirty-three hours out and I keep coming back to the same thought: the second leg of a two-legged tie is where character shows up or doesn't. We won at home with control and a set piece. Good. Now we go to them with a one-goal lead and they've had a week to think about what went wrong, how to fix it, and how their home crowd can push us into mistakes. That's not a comfortable position no matter how much we dominated the first ninety minutes.

The psychology matters more than the tactics tomorrow. A 1-0 lead is enough to make you nervous. You can't play for a draw at Zabrze when a draw sends it to extra time and penalties. You have to play to win. But you also can't get stretched trying to kill it off, because a team with nothing to lose finds spaces in stretched transitions. That's where they bit Lech three times. That's where they'll bite us if we're impatient.

Górnik's hoping we panic. They're hoping we come at them too early and too hard and leave gaps. What we actually need is patience with the ball, discipline without it, and someone who's done this before to hold the line when the heat gets real. We got it done in the first leg with a set piece goal, but tomorrow belongs to the defence.

I was at Saraçoğlu for the first leg. Quiet evening. We made it look routine. Tomorrow won't be routine. Tomorrow is where you actually find out if you're through or if you've given yourself a problem you could have avoided.
