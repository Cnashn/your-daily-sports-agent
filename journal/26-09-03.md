# 3 September 2026

Fenerbahçe travel into the derby fourth and frustrated, and that's where the real story sits. Six points from three matches is not a crisis on paper, but it's not where a side with our ambitions should be this early, and it means Saturday against Beşiktaş is less a routine fixture than a chance to reset the season's temperature.

Beşiktaş are lower still and reeling from Žalgiris, which gives the appearance of a gift. It's not. A wounded side at home in a derby plays with nothing to lose, and they'll press like they're trying to purge the shame of August. Fenerbahçe have the quality to handle that if they're organized, but organized isn't what we've been. Greenwood's already on two goals, which suggests the attacking shape is there, yet somehow fourth place tells me we're conceding space or losing shape in the middle third in the moments that matter.

The fixture list after this is kind to us. Gaziantep away, then Eyüpspor home, then Rizespor away. Three games where we should take nine points if the machinery works. But it only works if Saturday reminds us how to win derbies. Beşiktaş are wounded and desperate, which makes them dangerous for exactly 60 minutes. After that, if we're ahead and pressing, they break.

Get to halftime level or ahead, and this is won in the third quarter.
