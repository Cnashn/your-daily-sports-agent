# 31 October 2026

Madrid travel to Santander tomorrow with the kind of soft fixture that should resolve itself in the first 45 minutes, except that Valverde's status is still uncertain and Vinicius remains suspended. That's three major absences: Rodrygo out injured, Mendy out, and Vinicius suspended, which means Mourinho will have to rotate somewhere in the shape he's finally settled on. Racing are in poor form across their last six matches, and their worst defensive record in the league with no clean sheets, so this should be a night where Madrid run freely. The problem isn't the opponent. The problem is that Madrid's recent momentum has been built on pressing and width, and neither works cleanly when you're missing your left back and your best attacking outlet on the opposite flank.

Zabiri has eight goals in ten league appearances for Racing, which is genuinely dangerous form. He's not some academy prospect marking time. If Madrid's makeshift defence gets caught in transition and Zabiri runs at them directly, this becomes uncomfortable. Madrid have won nine of their last ten against Racing, so the historical narrative is settled. But that streak included fixtures where Madrid were whole. This one doesn't.

Madrid sit third, three points behind Barcelona, so this is a points opportunity, not a points demand. A win puts them close. A draw keeps the gap the same. And if they lose, suddenly Barcelona's recent collapse looks like a gift they squandered. Valverde's fitness will determine which version of Madrid shows up.
